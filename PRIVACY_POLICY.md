**Information Collection and Use**

The app has no user accounts and no server of mine. Most of the information below stays on your device or goes directly from it to Google. I receive only the error reports described under Log Data and, if you contact me by email, your email address and what you write.

*Location*

With your permission, the app uses your precise or approximate location to show where you are on the map and to calculate distances from your current position. It reads your location only while the map is on screen, never in the background. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in the Android settings, and still calculate distances between any points you choose on the map.

*Information sent to Google*

The app sends Google the following, so that the features you use can work:

*   Search: the text you search for.
*   Elevation profile: the coordinates of every point of the distance you draw or load. You can turn it off with "Show elevation profile" in the app's settings.
*   Distance details: the coordinates of the first and last points, to find their addresses.

If a distance starts at your current position, those coordinates include your location. Google also receives your IP address with each request. The map itself uses the Maps SDK for Android, which also sends Google a pseudonymous identifier, information about your device, errors in the SDK and how you interact with the map.

*Information kept on your device*

The app stores on your device the distances you save (their name, length, date and the coordinates of their points), your settings and the hints you have already seen. If Android backup is on, Android may copy this information to your Google account. See [how Android backup works](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2819582).

*Information you share*

When you share a distance, the app passes its addresses, coordinates and length to the app you choose.

*Third-party services*

The app uses these services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

*   Google Maps Platform (maps, search, addresses and elevation) and Google Play services (location): [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/)
*   Google Play (app reviews): [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   Firebase Crashlytics (error reports): [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

**Log Data**

When an error happens in the app, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report to Google, which I can read and use only to find and fix errors. The report includes details of the error, the state of the app, information about your device such as its model and operating system version, the date and time, and identifiers generated for this installation of the app. It can include log messages and, if a response from Google couldn't be read, part of that response, such as an address or coordinates. I don't add your name, your email address or any other identifier to the reports.

Firebase also receives basic information about the sessions of the app, such as when one starts, and about the device, to measure how often errors happen.

*   Error reports: Firebase Crashlytics keeps them, with their identifiers, for 90 days before starting to remove them.
