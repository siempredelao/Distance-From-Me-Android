Distance From Me measures the straight-line distance between places on a satellite map. Start from your current position or from any other place, and long press where you want to end.

Features:
- Measure from your current position or from any place on the map
- Add as many points as you need to measure a route made of straight lines
- Search for a place by its name or address
- See the elevation profile of the distance
- See the addresses and coordinates of its first and last points
- Save your distances with a name, and load them again later
- Share a distance with your friends
- Choose between kilometres and metres, or miles, yards and feet
- Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Catalan

Your location is only used if you allow it. Without it, you can still measure between any points on the map.

The app is open source and has no ads and no accounts.

It needs Google Play services and an internet connection.
